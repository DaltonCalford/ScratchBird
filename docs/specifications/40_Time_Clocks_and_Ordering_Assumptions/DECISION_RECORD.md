# Section 40 Decision Record

## Current authoritative decisions

Time in ScratchBird is primarily annotation, formatting, timeout policy, or explicit typed data unless an adjacent correctness-owning section says otherwise.

Transaction correctness, schema correctness, and replay correctness are not delegated to wall-clock semantics.

This section exists to prevent folklore about timestamp ordering, distributed clocks, leases, or locale behavior from silently becoming correctness claims.
