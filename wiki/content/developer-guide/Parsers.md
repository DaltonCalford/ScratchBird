# Parsers and Emulation

**Last Updated:** 2026-02-03

---

ScratchBird includes a native V2 parser and emulated parsers for PostgreSQL,
MySQL, and Firebird. Each parser handles SQL text, maps it to SBLR, and formats
results for the dialect.
